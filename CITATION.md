# References and Citations

This project uses several established computational prediction tools and publicly available databases. The following publications and resources should be cited when referring to the corresponding methods or data sources.

## 1. SIFT

Ng PC, Henikoff S. Predicting deleterious amino acid substitutions.

*Genome Research.* 2001;11(5):863–874.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.176601

SIFT predicts whether amino-acid substitutions are likely to affect protein function based primarily on sequence conservation and related sequence information.

## 2. PolyPhen-2

Adzhubei IA, Schmidt S, Peshkin L, et al. A method and server for predicting damaging missense mutations.

*Nature Methods.* 2010;7:248–249.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth0410-248

PolyPhen-2 was used to predict the potential functional and structural effects of amino-acid substitutions.

## 3. I-Mutant 2.0

Capriotti E, Fariselli P, Casadio R. I-Mutant2.0: predicting stability changes upon mutation from the protein sequence or structure.

*Nucleic Acids Research.* 2005;33(suppl_2):W306–W310.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gki375

I-Mutant 2.0 was used to estimate predicted changes in protein stability following individual amino-acid substitutions.

## 4. Project HOPE

Venselaar H, te Beek TAH, Kuipers RKP, Hekkelman ML, Vriend G. Protein structure analysis of mutations causing inheritable diseases. An e-Science approach with life scientist friendly interfaces.

*BMC Bioinformatics.* 2010;11:548.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-11-548

Project HOPE was used to investigate structural and physicochemical consequences of the selected PTEN variants.

## 5. ClinVar

Landrum MJ, Lee JM, Benson M, et al. ClinVar: public archive of interpretations of clinically relevant variants.

*Nucleic Acids Research.* 2016;44(D1):D862–D868.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkv1222

ClinVar was used as a source of clinical variant annotations for the selected PTEN variants.

## 6. Protein Sequence and Annotation Resources

The PTEN protein sequence and associated annotations used during the analysis were obtained from publicly available protein and gene databases as documented in the project dataset and methodology.
